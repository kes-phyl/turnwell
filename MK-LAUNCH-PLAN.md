## MK market intelligence

- **Rental stock:** Large modern build-to-rent + established Bletchley/Wolverton terraces. Mix of flats (Central MK) and 3–4 bed family homes (Shenley, Walton).
- **Checkout pressure:** Agents like Connells and Taylors use strict inventory standards — oven and bathroom limescale are common fail points.
- **Competitors:** Hello Services (budget), Housekeep (platform, ~£374 avg), endoftenancycleaning-miltonkeynes.co.uk (from £95 — race to bottom). **Your edge:** local, personal, agent-focused, photo-verified.
- **Do NOT join:** Bark, Checkatrade paid leads — MK is overserved with lead gen platforms.
